Quevano Weather sends the city you search to WeatherAPI.com to retrieve current conditions and forecast information. Weather data may be delayed, incomplete, inaccurate, or unavailable. It is for general information only and is not a substitute for official alerts, professional advice, or emergency services.

Availability and third-party services

The app depends on internet access and third-party services. Continuous availability is not guaranteed. WeatherAPI.com is a separate service subject to its own terms and policies.

Your responsibilities

Use the app lawfully and do not misuse, disrupt, or attempt to interfere with it or its providers. You are responsible for decisions made using weather information. Do not rely on the app for emergency or other safety-critical decisions.

Privacy and retention

City searches are sent to the app server and weather provider to return results. The app stores the accepted policy version in this browser's local storage. See the Privacy Policy draft for known data flows and unresolved questions.
